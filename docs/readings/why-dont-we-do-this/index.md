# Why Don't We Do This

A place for the gap between what you read and what your current project(s)
actually do — questions worth raising with yourself or the team.

## Format

```markdown
### 2026-09-15 — Title

**Source:** link

**Current practice:** what we do today.

**Alternative:** what the source suggests instead.

**Open question:** why aren't we doing that — cost, ignorance, or a good reason?
```
